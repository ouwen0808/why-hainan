🏝️ Hainan Quick Guide 海南快速指南

Essential facts for foreigners · 外籍人士必备要点 · Updated Aug 2026

Visa-Free Entry · 免签入境

  • 86 countries can enter Hainan visa-free for up to 30 days — for tourism, business, family visits, medical treatment, exhibitions & sports.86 国可免签入境海南，最长 30 天，限旅游/商务/探亲/医疗/会展/体育。
  • Valid ports: Haikou Meilan, Sanya Phoenix, Bo'ao, Yangpu, Haikou Port.口岸：海口美兰、三亚凤凰、博鳌、洋浦港、海口港。
  • 144-hour transit via Hong Kong & Macau; 15-day cruise visa-free.港澳 144 小时过境；邮轮 15 天免签。

Tax · 税收

  • Qualified talent pays a max 15% personal income tax (vs 45% mainland).合格人才个税封顶 15%（内地为 45%）。
  • Most imports are zero-tariff in the Free Trade Port.自贸港多数进口商品零关税。

Payments · 支付

  • Link Visa / Mastercard / JCB / Amex to WeChat Pay or Alipay.微信/支付宝可绑定 Visa、Mastercard、JCB、Amex。
  • Foreign phone numbers are supported. Alipay: no fee under ¥200; above that 3%.支持境外手机号；支付宝 200 元内免手续费，以上收 3%。
  • e-CNY App supports 210+ countries/regions phone registration.数字人民币 e-CNY 支持 210+ 国家/地区手机号注册。

Renting · 租房

  • Verify the agent's licence, the property certificate & owner ID before signing.签约前核实中介资质、房产证与产权人身份。
  • Watch for sub-letting (二房东) scams; confirm the deposit & penalty terms in the contract.警惕二房东；合同明确押金与违约金条款。
  • Foreigners must register the lease at the local police station.外籍人士须到派出所办理租赁登记。

Healthcare · 就医

  • 74+ hospitals offer international service; Haikou People's Hospital & Sanya People's Hospital have dedicated international departments.74+ 家医院提供国际服务；海口、三亚市人民医院设国际医疗部。
  • Sanya intl clinic: 0898-88250332.三亚国际门诊：0898-88250332。
  • Buy commercial medical/accident insurance recommended.建议购买商业医疗/意外保险。
