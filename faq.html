Frequently Asked Questions · 常见问题

Q: "86 countries visa-free" — is that right?问：「86 国免签」——对吗？
No. As of the latest NIA announcement, 61 countries/regions enjoy visa-free entry to Hainan for ordinary passport holders. Entry is for up to 30 days, counted from 00:00 of the day after entry, and applies only within Hainan's administrative region.
不对。根据国家移民管理局最新公告，61 个国家/地区的普通护照持有人可免签进入海南，最长 30 天（自入境次日零时起算），且仅限海南行政区域内。
Q: What activities does visa-free entry cover?问：免签入境适用哪些事由？
Tourism, business, family visits, medical treatment, exhibitions, and sports. Work and study require separate visas/permits.
旅游、商贸、访问、探亲、就医、会展、体育。工作、学习须另行办理签证/许可。
Q: Can I travel beyond Hainan on visa-free entry?问：免签入境后能去海南以外吗？
No. Visa-free entry for Hainan is restricted to Hainan. Travel to mainland China requires a separate, valid visa.
不能。海南免签仅限海南，前往内地须另行办理有效签证。
Q: "15% income tax cap" — does everyone get it?问：「15% 个税封顶」——人人适用吗？
No. The 15% effective rate applies to high-end and urgently-needed talent on the official list who meet conditions (e.g. 183-day residency rule, actual stay of 90+ days in Hainan). The portion of income tax above 15% is waived — it is not a flat rate for all residents.
不。15% 实际税负针对纳入清单且符合条件的高端紧缺人才（如 183 天居住规则、在琼实际居住满 90 天等）。免征的是超出 15% 的部分，并非对所有居民统一适用。
Q: "Zero tariffs" — on everything?问：「零关税」——所有商品吗？
No. Zero-tariff applies only to goods on the positive lists, purchased at designated outlets, within annual quotas. It is not a blanket exemption on all imports.
不。零关税仅限正面清单内商品，在指定经营场所购买，且受年度额度限制，并非所有进口商品一律免税。
Q: Which hospitals offer international service?问：哪些医院提供国际服务？
Institutions across 18 cities/counties and 31 facilities have set up international outpatient clinics. Haikou and Sanya People's Hospitals have dedicated international departments.
18 个市县、31 家机构设国际门诊。海口、三亚市人民医院设专门国际医疗部。
Q: Can I pay with my foreign card?问：能用外卡支付吗？
Yes — link Visa / Mastercard / JCB / Amex to WeChat Pay or Alipay. Foreign phone numbers are supported. Alipay is fee-free under ¥200 and charges 3% above that. e-CNY App supports 210+ countries/regions registration.
可以——微信/支付宝可绑定 Visa、Mastercard、JCB、Amex，支持境外手机号。支付宝 200 元内免手续费，以上收 3%。数字人民币 e-CNY 支持 210+ 国家/地区注册。
Q: I'm moving to Hainan — where do I start?问：我要搬到海南——从哪里开始？
Register your residence at the local police station after arrival, get a local SIM card, and open a bank account. For work, apply for a Z visa and work permit before entry. See our Daily Guide for step-by-step help.
抵达后到派出所办理住宿登记，办理本地手机卡并开立银行账户。工作须入境前办好 Z 签证与工作许可。详见每日指南。
